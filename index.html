Synthetic Biologist

My academic life from 2010–2022.

Harvard Systems Biology

I earned my PhD in Systems Biology from Harvard Medical School, working in Pam Silver's lab. My thesis focused on engineering microbial metabolism — designing synthetic circuits that let bacteria produce useful compounds more efficiently.

Université Paris Cité

From 2010–2021 I was a postdoc and research group leader at the CRI in Paris. My lab studied antimicrobial resistance and built tools to make drug discovery cheaper, using engineered E. coli as a screening platform.

Synthetic Biology One

In 2018 I created a 61-episode YouTube series teaching core techniques and concepts in synthetic biology. As of 2025 it has 20,000+ subscribers and 1 million+ total views.
